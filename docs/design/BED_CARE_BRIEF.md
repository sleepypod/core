# Bed-care surface brief

Mode: Operate, with recovery guidance in Read mode. This is an extension of the
[existing product](../../PRODUCT.md), with no replacement design system or new comp.

- **Thesis:** help someone identify an actual maintenance concern and reach the
  relevant diagnostic without interpreting raw service data.
- **Existing world:** preserve AppShell/Theme, light and dark surfaces, system
  navigation and the established typography. Avoid decorative health scores.
- **Story:** current check time → five distinct observations → relevant next step
  → optional evidence or recovery guidance.
- **First viewport:** a recognizable bed-care title, a short explanation, refresh
  control and the first water observation. Global navigation remains usable.
- **Form:** divided observations in one restrained surface, disclosures for
  secondary detail and standard links. Native controls support Dynamic Type.

The quality bar is accurate freshness and advice, accessible diagnostic links,
readable narrow/wide and phone/tablet layouts, explicit practice mode and no implied
physical verification. Review complete viewports as well as recovery states; a
panel crop alone cannot establish that fixed navigation leaves links reachable.
