# Comfort comparisons inside the existing app

Mode: Operate. This adds a section to owner Autopilot → Learning and the personal
workspace. It preserves their existing light/dark surfaces, text hierarchy,
control shapes and colors; it does not replace either visual system.

The everyday question is whether a nearby overnight setting feels better. A person
chooses a temperature inside their existing range and an earliest bedtime date.
The server chooses eligible scheduled nights and returns an immutable review.
The first review view leads with both temperatures, the actual date span and the
preserved wake plan. Every settling/comparison night and skipped date is available
in the adjacent disclosure. Saving is explicitly planning-only until a qualified
controller exists. It does not change the current routine.

Saved comparisons expose Stop, the actual evidence conclusion and explicit morning
feedback. Missing exposure cannot become a successful treatment merely because a
person supplied a positive comfort rating. Exclusion reasons remain readable.
Stopping the saved comparison is distinct from the app's hardware Stop.

Owner and personal sessions share the renderer but never the transport, scope or
library. Private content is memory-only and cleared on backgrounding, account
change, maintenance and unmount. Expired reviews cannot submit; uncertain writes
offer a read of the saved result, never an automatic retry. Back preserves the
chosen date and temperature within the current visible workspace.

Required rendered checks: narrow phone in dark appearance with enlarged text,
tablet in light appearance, compact review and expanded nights, saved/Stop state,
uncertain-save recovery and the personal feedback flow. Existing personal learning
captures and current owner design tokens are the visual references. Browser
fixtures are synthetic and are not overnight or physical qualification evidence.
