# Temporary household reviews

Mode: Operate. This is a local extension of the existing personal household workspace.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Prepare a guest visit or a short side swap with each person's own settings visible and their private records protected. Reviewing is distinct from switching the bed.

**OWN-WORLD:** Preserve the workspace's system typography, light/dark neutral surfaces, blue primary controls, restrained dividers and existing two-zone bed diagram. No new visual system or decorative imagery.

**STORY:** Check the current plan, compare your current and proposed side and operating week, then save your independent approval. The owner can prepare a plan; participants can cancel it. Saved approvals never activate hardware.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A plain heading, persistent review-only explanation and refresh action precede the two-zone comparison. Status and intended end are adjacent. The primary action follows the required personal review acknowledgments.

**FORM:** An inline comparison and compact form, extending the established arrangement review. No concept seed is needed for this local extension. The signature interaction expands each night without revealing a partner's routine. No entrance animation; reduced motion retains all content.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

The final design check compares with the incumbent workspace; ordinary extensions preserve its system files. This work adds no shipping raster or DESIGN.md replacement.

## Product boundaries

- Save and approve only a private review. Activation and return are not available in this release.
- Show capability-disabled, loading, empty, current, expired, changed, cancelled, lost-reply and account-change states truthfully.
- A guest interval would withhold personal recordings, pause learning and keep shared house actions quiet. A return needs a fresh review, never an automatic timer rollback.
- Display actual operating nights and timezone; do not substitute unpublished drafts.
- No credentials, private plan content or partner schedules in browser storage. Hidden or changed-account pages clear private content.
- Verification: responsive phone/tablet/desktop, light/dark, large text, keyboard, no unintended writes, loss/recovery and strict transport scope.
